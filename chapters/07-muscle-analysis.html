7  Rodent Muscle Analysis

7.1 Introduction

While inverse kinematics and inverse dynamics analyses can provide insight into the overall movement and forces at the joints, they do not directly inform us about the behavior of individual muscles. To gain a deeper understanding of muscle function during movement, we can utilize muscle analysis tools in OpenSim like computed muscle control (CMC). This allows us to estimate muscle lengths, forces, and activations based on the kinematics and kinetics of the movement.

7.1.1 Computed Muscle Control (CMC)

CMC is an optimization-based algorithm that computes muscle excitations required to produce a desired movement. It takes into account the muscle’s force-length and force-velocity properties, as well as the geometry of the musculoskeletal model.
